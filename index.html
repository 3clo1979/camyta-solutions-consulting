Scope & project planning+

Define the work before assumptions become commitments. Establish a practical baseline for decisions, effort, and delivery.

  • Statements of work and scope boundaries
  • Effort estimates with documented assumptions
  • Dependencies and delivery checkpoints
  • Risk and responsibility planning
YOU LEAVE WITH A plan with visible boundaries.
Solution architecture+

Connect business requirements to a solution that fits the surrounding systems and the people who will support it.

  • Solution design and architecture diagrams
  • Workflow and content management design
  • Integration requirements and data flows
  • Technical options and tradeoff decisions
YOU LEAVE WITH A practical, explainable design.
Project & vendor leadership+

Keep people, priorities, and decisions connected while the work moves forward. Coordinate within an explicitly agreed role and capacity.

  • Stakeholder and vendor coordination
  • Delivery planning and status communication
  • Risks, issues, and dependency tracking
  • Change assessment and decision support
YOU LEAVE WITH Clear ownership and informed next steps.
Testing & transition+

Validate the solution against the original need. Prepare the business and support teams to take ownership of what comes next.

  • User acceptance testing plans and scenarios

  • Readiness reviews and open-issue tracking
  • Documentation and knowledge transfer
YOU LEAVE WITH A more informed handoff.
